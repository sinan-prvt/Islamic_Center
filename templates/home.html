Welcome to SKSSF Puthupparamba

Samastha Kerala Sunni Students Federation (SKSSF) Puthupparamba Unit is a vibrant community organization dedicated to the holistic development of students and the wider society.

Through our continuous efforts in religious education, social relief activities, and youth empowerment programs, we strive to uphold the rich Islamic traditions while equipping our youth for the modern world.
