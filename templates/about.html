About Samastha Kerala Sunni Students Federation (SKSSF)

The Samastha Kerala Sunni Students Federation (SKSSF) is a prominent Islamic student organization in India, serving as the higher student wing of the Samastha Kerala Jamiyyathul Ulama. Founded on February 19, 1989, in Kozhikode, Kerala, the organization operates under the noble motto: "Knowledge, Humility, and Service."

SKSSF aims to bring Muslim students under its fold to nurture them into a righteous moral life and to provide leadership and support in educational, spiritual, and community-related issues across the nation.

SKSSF Puthupparamba Unit

The Puthupparamba Unit is one of the most active local branches, deeply committed to working at the grassroots level in our community. For years, we have been a pillar of support, promoting Islamic education, organizing relief activities, and empowering the youth.

As an ancillary unit of the state organization, we strive to bring the broader vision of SKSSF to life within Puthupparamba through community service, educational dars, and spiritual guidance.

👁️ Our Vision

To empower the youth with authentic Islamic knowledge and modern education, building a generation of morally upright leaders.

🎯 Our Mission

To organize educational classes, conduct monthly spiritual gatherings, and coordinate comprehensive relief efforts to support the needy in our community.
