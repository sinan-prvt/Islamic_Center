ABOUT US

A movement of faith, knowledge and service.

പുതുപ്പറമ്പ് യൂണിറ്റിലെ എസ്.കെ.എസ്.എസ്.എഫ്.

Founded in 1989 as the student wing of Samastha Kerala Jam'iyyaathul Ulama, SKSSF has grown into one of the largest Islamic student movements in South India. The Puthupparamba unit continues this proud legacy at the roots.

Faith-Driven

Rooted in the Ahlus Sunnah wal Jama'ah tradition passed down for generations.

Knowledge-First

From madrasas to universities – empowering minds with authentic learning.

Service-Oriented

Standing with the community through relief, education and mentorship.

OUR PEOPLE

Building leaders of tomorrow

35+ YEARS OF
LEGACY

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To empower the youth with authentic Islamic knowledge and modern secular education, nurturing a morally upright, self-reliant generation equipped to lead society with humility and integrity.

Our Mission

To organize regular spiritual gatherings, coordinate medical and economic relief for the needy, support underprivileged students, and foster unity across the community.

OUR ORGANIZATIONAL STRUCTURE

Specialized Wings

🎓

TREND

Education & Career

Dedicated education and career guidance wing. TREND helps students navigate academic paths, entrance exams, and professional development with clarity.

🤝

VIKHAYA

Relief & Volunteer Service

Humanitarian and relief wing providing medical support, blood donation drives, emergency disaster response, and community assistance.

📖

IBAD

Propagation & Values

Dawah and spiritual orientation wing focused on promoting traditional Islamic values, moral guidance, and weekly dars programs.
